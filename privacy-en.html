Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Hako Games
Türkçe English

Hako Games ("we", "us", "our") respects the privacy of SnapSlide ("the App") users. This policy explains what data is processed when you use the App, how it is used, and what rights you have.

1. Data We Collect

Locally stored data

Your game progress — completed level number, difficulty preference, and language selection — is stored solely in your device's local storage. This data is never transmitted to our servers, is inaccessible to us, and is deleted from your device when you uninstall the App.

No personal data collection

We do not request, collect, or store any directly identifying personal information such as name, email address, phone number, or precise location data. No account registration is required to use the App.

2. Advertising Services

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising platform provided by Google LLC. AdMob may process the following data to deliver interest-based advertising:

  • Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA)
  • Device model and operating system version
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (country or city level)
  • Ad impression and interaction data

This data is processed under Google's own privacy policy. For detailed information: policies.google.com/privacy

You may reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

3. European Users (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, your explicit consent for personalized advertising is requested on first launch of the App. You may withdraw your consent at any time.

Under the GDPR you have the following rights:

  • Right of access to your processed data
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure of your data
  • Right to object to data processing
  • Right to data portability

4. Children's Privacy

The App is directed at a general audience and is not specifically marketed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided data, please contact us at the address below; the relevant data will be deleted promptly.

5. Data Security

Because the App does not store user data on any server, the risk of a data breach is minimal. The security practices of third-party service providers (Google AdMob) remain their own responsibility.

6. Third-Party Links

Advertisements displayed in the App may direct you to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these sites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of every site you visit.

7. Policy Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be announced within the App and the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the App after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
